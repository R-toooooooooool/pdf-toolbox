快速上手

  1. 把 PDF（或照片）拖進視窗，或按各分頁的「加入」「開啟 PDF」。
  2. 拉進來一次，五個分頁都能用，不用重選；加字、擷取、複製文字上方的下拉選單可以切換檔案。
  3. 做完按存檔或 Ctrl+S：Chrome、Edge 會跳出「另存新檔」視窗，自己選資料夾（也可以直接蓋掉原檔）；其他瀏覽器存到「下載」資料夾。不想跳視窗，到右上「📁 檔名 ⚙」取消勾選。
🔒 所有處理都在你自己的瀏覽器裡完成，PDF 不會上傳到任何地方。只有在「回報區」發的回報、投票和留言會送到網路上。

各功能怎麼用

合併・分割・裁半

  • 一次拖進好幾個檔就能合併；拖拉縮圖排順序，點一下選取，雙擊放大看，按住 Shift 點可以一次選一段。
  • 「裁成兩半」：A3 一張印兩頁的掃描檔，可以切成左右（或上下）兩頁，中線位置可調。沒選頁時會套用全部頁。
  • 存檔四種：存成一份、只存選取的頁、每頁一個檔（壓縮檔）、依頁碼拆（例如輸入 1-3, 4-8）。弄錯了按「↶ 復原」。

加字

  • 點頁面任何位置就能打字，可選正黑體、標楷體、新細明體、字級、顏色、粗體。拖左上角 ✥ 移動。
  • 「🖍 螢光筆」：拖過要標記的字，底下的字還看得到。可選顏色、粗細；畫法選「直線」（自動拉平）或「自由畫」。
  • 「方框、圓圈、直線、箭頭」：拖一下就畫好，圈重點、指位置用。可選顏色、粗細、實線或虛線。點一下選取後可以拖動、拉圓點調整，按 Delete 刪除。
  • 「白底遮蓋」：拖出白色方框蓋掉錯字，再在上面加正確的字（只是蓋住，原本的字還在）。
  • 「⬛ 塗銷」：遮機密資料用，可選黑色或白色（白色看起來像空白、比較不突兀）。存檔時那一頁會整頁轉成圖片，框底下的字真的被刪掉，複製、搜尋都找不到。

浮水印

  • 「➕ 加浮水印」：打字（或按「機密文件」「草稿」這些常用字）或選公司 logo 圖片，調大小、濃淡、角度，可以只放中間一個或滿版重複。畫面上看到的就是存出來的樣子。
  • 可以選全部頁、只有目前這頁、或自訂頁碼（例如 1-3, 5）。勾「防止別人去掉」會整頁轉成圖片，別人很難去掉，但字不能再選取。
  • 「➖ 去除浮水印」：能去掉 Acrobat、Word、本工具加的浮水印；掃描檔或直接畫在內容裡的去不掉（改用「加字」的白底遮蓋）。只用在公司自己的文件。

擷取畫面

  • 在頁面上拖出範圍 → 「複製圖片」直接貼到 LINE、Word、Email，或下載成圖片。要印出來請把清晰度選「很清楚」。

複製文字

  • 取一頁、全部頁，或在頁面上拖框只取框內的字；斷行、多餘空格會自動整理。
  • 掃描檔（圖片）沒有文字，會自動辨識上面的字；第一次用要下載辨識資料，稍等一下。

文字整理

  • 照畫面上的 ①②③ 做：① 把 PDF 複製的文字貼到左邊（或按「📄 取出目前 PDF 的文字」）→ ② 中間選整理方式，不知道選哪個就用「一般文件」→ ③ 右邊按「📋 複製結果」，再到 Word、LINE 貼上。
  • 第一次用可以先按「👀 先看範例」看整理前後的差別。細項設定收在中間下面，看不懂可以不用管。
  • 熟了以後：「⚡ 一鍵整理剪貼簿」＝讀剪貼簿、整理完直接放回剪貼簿，回去按 Ctrl+V 就好。

常錯字對照表

  • 掃描檔常認錯的字，在結果裡選取 → 按「加入常錯字」（複製文字分頁叫「選取的字加入常錯字」）→ 輸入正確的字。以後會自動改正。

命名規則

  • 右上角「📁 檔名」選這次存檔要用哪一組規則；按 ⚙ 新增自己的規則，例如 {日期}-{自訂}-{序號} → 20261028-業務部-001.pdf。
  • 每組規則的序號分開算，每天從 001 開始。

常見問題

畫面上方出現「工具載入失敗」

這個工具第一次打開要從網路下載，請確認有連上網路，再按 F5 重新整理。

加上去的字為什麼不能選取、複製？

為了保證中文在任何電腦、手機打開都不會變亂碼或方框，加的字是用高解析度「印」在頁面上。代價是之後沒辦法再選取這些字。

白底遮蓋可以拿來遮機密資料嗎？

不行。白底只是蓋住看不到，底下原本的字還在，別人用複製或搜尋還是找得到。機密資料請改用「⬛ 塗銷」（可選黑色或白色）：存檔時有塗銷的頁會整頁轉成圖片，框底下的字真的刪掉；代價是那幾頁其他的字也不能再選取、複製。存好後可以用「複製文字」分頁檢查一次。

掃描檔認出來的字有錯怎麼辦？

1. 掃描時選高畫質、頁面放正；2. 只框選需要的那段，避開表格線和印章；3. 常認錯的字加進「常錯字對照表」，以後自動改；4. 重要文件可用 Windows 剪取工具（Win＋Shift＋S →「文字動作」）再辨識一次對照。手寫字基本上認不出來。

有密碼或有保護的 PDF 可以用嗎？

有「開啟密碼」的會請你輸入密碼。只有「禁止編輯」保護的 PDF，在「合併・分割・裁半」存檔時會改用圖片方式存（文字不能再選取），「加字」則無法使用。

存好的檔案在哪裡？檔名可以改嗎？

Chrome、Edge 存檔時會跳出「另存新檔」視窗，存在你選的資料夾，檔名可以直接改（預設照右上角的命名規則）。不想跳視窗：到右上「📁 檔名 ⚙」取消「跳出另存新檔視窗」，就會存到瀏覽器的「下載」資料夾。

換電腦或清除瀏覽器資料後，對照表和命名規則不見了

這兩樣存在你這台電腦的瀏覽器裡。平常記得按「匯出」備份，換電腦後用「匯入」就能還原，也可以把檔案傳給同事匯入。

手機可以用嗎？

可以開、可以合併拆檔、擷取、取字；加字和框選這類精細動作用電腦會比較順手。

Getting started

  1. Drag PDFs (or photos) into the window, or use “Add” / “Open PDF” in each tab.
  2. Add a file once and every tab can use it — no need to pick it again. In Add text, Snapshot and Copy text, switch files from the drop-down at the top.
  3. When you’re done, click save or press Ctrl+S. Chrome and Edge show a “Save as” window so you can choose the folder (you can also overwrite the original); other browsers save to your Downloads folder. To skip the window, untick it under “📁 File name ⚙” at the top right.
🔒 Everything happens inside your own browser — your PDFs are never uploaded. Only the reports, votes and comments you post in “Feedback” are sent over the internet.

How each tab works

Merge · Split · Halve

  • Drop several files at once to merge them. Drag thumbnails to reorder, click to select, double-click to enlarge, and Shift+click to select a range.
  • “Cut in half”: split scans with two pages on one sheet (e.g. A3) into left and right (or top and bottom) pages. The cut position is adjustable. With no pages selected it applies to all pages.
  • Four ways to save: one PDF, selected pages only, one file per page (zip), or by page ranges (e.g. 1-3, 4-8). Made a mistake? Click “↶ Undo”.

Add text

  • Click anywhere on the page to type. Choose the font, size, color and bold. Drag ✥ at the top left to move it.
  • “🖍 Highlighter”: drag over text to mark it — the text stays visible. Choose the color and width; “Straight” keeps the stroke level, “Freehand” lets you draw freely.
  • “Box, Circle, Line, Arrow”: just drag to draw — great for marking key points. Choose the color, width and solid or dashed. Click one to select it, then drag to move, drag the dots to adjust, or press Delete.
  • “White-out”: drag a white box over a mistake, then type the correct text on top (it only covers it — the original text is still there).
  • “⬛ Redact”: for confidential data, in black or white (white looks like blank space). When saved, that page is turned into an image and the text under the box is really deleted — it can’t be copied or searched.

Watermark

  • “➕ Add watermark”: type text (or click a common one like “CONFIDENTIAL” or “DRAFT”) or choose a logo image, then adjust size, opacity and angle — one in the center or tiled across the page. What you see is what gets saved.
  • Apply it to all pages, the current page, or custom pages (e.g. 1-3, 5). Ticking “Prevent removal” turns the pages into images so the watermark is hard to remove, but the text can no longer be selected.
  • “➖ Remove watermark”: removes watermarks added by Acrobat, Word or this tool. Watermarks in scanned files or drawn into the content can’t be removed (use White-out in “Add text” instead). Only use this on your own documents.

Snapshot

  • Drag an area on the page → “Copy image” to paste it straight into LINE, Word or email, or download it as an image. For printing, set the quality to “Very sharp”.

Copy text

  • Extract one page, all pages, or drag a box to take only the text inside it. Broken lines and extra spaces are cleaned up automatically.
  • Scanned pages (images) have no text, so the words on them are recognized automatically. The first time, the recognition data needs to download — give it a moment.

Text cleanup

  • Follow ① ② ③ on screen: ① paste text copied from a PDF on the left (or click “📄 Extract text from the current PDF”) → ② choose a mode in the middle — if unsure, use “Documents” → ③ click “📋 Copy result” on the right and paste it into Word or LINE.
  • First time? Click “👀 Try an example” to see the before and after. The detailed settings are tucked away below the modes — you can ignore them.
  • Once you’re used to it: “⚡ Clean clipboard in one click” reads the clipboard, cleans it and puts it back — just press Ctrl+V.

Fix list

  • For words that scans often get wrong: select it in the result → click “Add to fix list” (in Copy text: “Add selection to fix list”) → type the correct text. It will be corrected automatically from then on.

Naming rules

  • Pick which rule to use under “📁 File name” at the top right; click ⚙ to create your own, e.g. {date}-{custom}-{no} → 20261028-Sales-001.pdf.
  • Each rule has its own number, which starts again at 001 every day.

FAQ

“The tool failed to load” appears at the top

The tool downloads its components the first time it opens. Check your internet connection and press F5 to reload.

Why can’t I select or copy the text I added?

To make sure Chinese text never turns into garbled characters or boxes on any computer or phone, added text is “printed” onto the page at high resolution. The trade-off is that it can’t be selected later.

Can I use White-out to hide confidential data?

No. White-out only covers it up — the original text is still underneath and can be copied or searched. Use “⬛ Redact” (black or white) instead: when saved, redacted pages become images and the text under the box is really deleted. The trade-off is that other text on those pages can’t be selected or copied. After saving, you can check it in the “Copy text” tab.

The recognized text from a scan has mistakes

1. Scan at high quality and keep the page straight; 2. select only the part you need, avoiding table lines and stamps; 3. add frequent mistakes to the “Fix list” so they’re corrected automatically; 4. for important documents, recognize the text again with the Windows Snipping Tool (Win+Shift+S → “Text actions”) and compare. Handwriting generally can’t be recognized.

Can I use password-protected PDFs?

For PDFs with an “open password”, you’ll be asked for the password. PDFs protected only against editing are saved as images in “Merge · Split · Halve” (text can’t be selected afterwards), and “Add text” can’t be used on them.

Where are my saved files? Can I change the name?

Chrome and Edge show a “Save as” window, so files go to the folder you choose and you can edit the name (the default follows the naming rule at the top right). To skip the window, untick “Show a Save as window” under “📁 File name ⚙” and files will go to your browser’s Downloads folder.

My fix list and naming rules disappeared after changing computers or clearing browser data

Both are stored in this computer’s browser. Use “Export” to back them up now and then, and “Import” to restore them on another computer — you can also share the file with colleagues.

Does it work on phones?

Yes — you can open files, merge and split, take snapshots and extract text. Precise tasks like adding text and selecting areas are easier on a computer.
